# The Privacy Policy page leads to 404 Not Found

## Urgency
HIGH

## Description
When the user tries to access the privacy policy page through the footer, they find themselves on a 404 Not Found page. This has been found on various mobiles and laptops.

## Devices and Environments
These are some of the reported devices and environments that are experiencing this issue.
| Device Type | Company / Model           | Operating System | Browser          | Browser Version | Reported On             |
| ----------- | ------------------------- | ---------------- | ---------------- | --------------- | ----------------------- |
| Mobile      | Apple iPhone 16 Pro       | iOS 26           | Safari           | 26.0            | Sep 3, 2026 – 9:42 AM   |
| Laptop      | Dell XPS 14               | Windows 11       | Google Chrome    | 140.0.7339.81   | Sep 5, 2026 – 2:17 PM   |
| Mobile      | Samsung Galaxy S25        | Android 16       | Google Chrome    | 140.0.7339.51   | Sep 8, 2026 – 11:05 AM  |
| Laptop      | Apple MacBook Air M4      | macOS 26         | Safari           | 26.0            | Sep 10, 2026 – 4:36 PM  |


## Impacted Users
Very likely: All users who are trying to access the privacy policy are impacted.

## Possible Fixes
- Ensure a privacy policy page file and related files exist and are deployed to public.
- Ensure that requests are correctly routed from the website footer to the privacy policy subpage

## Steps to test for success
1. Go to the main public website
2. Scroll all the way down to the footer
3. Click on the Privacy Policy hyperlink
4. Access to Privacy Policy
